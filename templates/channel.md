# channel.md - the comedy channel brief (reference for the skills and for Claude)

Niche: funny AI animated comedy - original characters, family-friendly, English (US first).
Formats: Shorts (30-55 s, vertical) daily-ish; one 2-4 minute horizontal episode a week.
Every video: a hook in the first 3 seconds, one clear comic situation, escalation, an unexpected twist, a
quick button line. Clean jokes. No real people, brands or other creators' characters.
Recurring cast: Biscuit (dramatic royal-minded orange cat), Waffles (overconfident detective pug), Nugget
(hyperactive puppy), Gary (tired office worker), Boss Hoot (buzzword owl manager), Narrator.
Running themes: pets vs. everyday tech, office absurdity, family chaos, food crimes, misunderstandings.
Never: clickbait the video does not deliver, fake numbers, mean-spirited humour, copying trends verbatim.
The platform's Channel Settings page is the live version of this brief.
